YOUR PRIVACY CHOICES

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

  • For iOS devices: Go to Settings > Privacy > Tracking and disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  • For Android devices: Go to Settings > Google > Ads and enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization"

Access and Control

You can access and control your personal information through the Application settings, where you can:

  • Update or correct your account information

  • Adjust your notification preferences
  • Manage your privacy settings

GDPR (European Users)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You can choose whether to consent to the processing of your personal data for personalized advertising. If you do not consent, you will still see ads, but they may not be relevant to you.

Additionally, you have the rights to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request erasure of your data
  • Restrict or object to the processing of your data
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided at the end of this policy.

CCPA (California Residents)

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). You can opt out of the "sale" of your personal information, which may include sharing certain data with our advertising partners. You also have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is being collected
  • Know if your personal information is sold or disclosed and to whom
  • Opt out of the sale of your personal information
  • Access your personal information
  • Request deletion of your personal information

COPPA (Children's Privacy)

Our Application is not directed to children under 13 years of age. If you are under 13, you can indicate this in the privacy settings, and we will implement appropriate restrictions as required by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will promptly delete it.
