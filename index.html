Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 27, 2025

Welcome to Stauw ID. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our application.

By using the Stauw ID, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1. Information You Provide

We do not require you to create an account or directly provide personal information to use our basic services.

1.2. Automatically Collected Information

When you use our application, we may automatically collect:

  • Device Information: Including device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, mobile network information, and hardware specifications.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous data about how you interact with our app, such as which features you use, frequency and duration of use, music playback patterns, and app launch count.
  • Error Logs: Information to help identify and troubleshoot app crashes and technical issues.
  • Advertising ID: The app collects Google's Advertising ID (GAID) to serve personalized ads when you consent to this feature.

2. Permissions We Request

Our application requires the following permissions to provide you with a seamless and feature-rich experience:

  • Internet Access: Essential for streaming music content, downloading songs, fetching playlists, displaying advertisements, and sending anonymous analytical data to improve our services.
  • Notification Access: Enables us to display interactive music player controls in your notification area while playing music, allowing you to control playback without opening the app.
  • Foreground Service: Powers continuous music playback when the app is minimized or when your device is locked, ensuring uninterrupted listening experience.
  • Advertising ID Access: Allows us to serve relevant advertisements based on your preferences and comply with advertising regulations. This identifier helps us deliver ads that may be of interest to you when personalization is enabled.
  • Media Access (Android 13+): Provides secure access to audio media on devices running Android 13 or higher, enabling advanced playback features and media library integration.
  • Network State: Allows the app to detect when your network connection changes to adapt streaming quality accordingly and switch between online and offline modes.
  • Storage Access: Required for downloading and saving music for offline playback, ensuring you can enjoy your favorite songs without an internet connection.

You can manage these permissions through your device settings at any time. Please note that restricting certain core permissions may limit app functionality or prevent specific features from working properly. We only request permissions that are necessary for delivering the features you expect from our music streaming application.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Provide and Maintain Services: To deliver the core functionality of our music streaming app and make improvements based on usage patterns.
  • Personalize User Experience: To understand how you use our app and optimize its features for better usability.
  • Display Advertisements: To show relevant advertisements that help support our free app and services. This includes personalized ads when you consent to them.
  • Analytics: To analyze usage patterns and improve app performance, content, and features.
  • Service Management: To manage the frequency and timing of advertisements to enhance user experience.
  • Communication: To respond to your inquiries or service-related issues.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws and regulations, including those related to privacy and data protection.

4. Advertising

4.1. Ad Services Implementation

Our application displays advertisements through Google AdMob and related Google advertising services. These platforms may utilize various technologies such as cookies, device identifiers, and tracking pixels to collect information about your application usage patterns and preferences. This data collection is aimed at delivering more relevant advertisements that align with your interests when personalization is enabled.

4.2. Ad Types, Formats and Frequency

The application displays several types of advertisements to support our free service model:

  • App Open Ads: These may appear briefly when you launch the application after a period of inactivity (typically 4+ hours). These ads are designed to be minimally intrusive during the app loading process.
  • Banner Ads: These non-intrusive advertisements appear in designated areas within the application interface, typically at the bottom of the screen, and do not interrupt your music listening experience.
  • Interstitial Ads: These full-screen advertisements may appear after playing multiple songs (approximately every 10 songs) and offer virtual rewards or extended ad-free periods in exchange for viewing the complete advertisement.

We have carefully implemented intelligent frequency capping and user experience optimization measures to ensure that advertisement display frequency remains reasonable and does not significantly impact your enjoyment of the application. Our ad serving logic is designed to balance user experience with the sustainability of our free service.

4.3. Ad Personalization Controls

During the initial application setup process, you are provided with clear options to choose whether to allow personalized advertisements based on your interests, app usage patterns, and device information. You have complete control over this setting and can modify your ad personalization preferences at any time through the application's privacy settings menu. Disabling personalization will result in non-targeted advertisements that are not based on your personal interests or usage behavior.

4.4. Third-party Ad Networks and Compliance

Our advertising implementation complies with all relevant data protection regulations, including GDPR, CCPA, and industry best practices. For comprehensive information about how Google processes and utilizes data for advertising purposes, please visit Google's Privacy & Terms for Partners and Google's Privacy Policy. We do not share your personal information with advertisers beyond what is necessary for the standard operation of mobile advertising services.
